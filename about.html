My Story

Hi, I Am Khai Rambo.
Your Trusted Advisor for Life.

I am a Senior Associate Director at PropNex Realty Pte Ltd — Singapore's largest real estate agency — with CEA Registration No. R056070J. My journey in property began over a decade ago, preceded by 10 years in the banking and finance industry, which gave me a unique edge in understanding the financial dimensions of every property decision.

Coming from a humble background, I understand what it means to want something better for your family. That personal drive has shaped how I approach every client — not as a transaction to close, but as a family whose future I am entrusted to protect and grow. I deeply yearn to help clients — especially those who believe they cannot achieve their goals — discover what is truly possible.

In just 3 years, I myself upgraded from HDB and now own two condominium apartments — walking the same path I guide my clients through. This first-hand experience means I understand every anxiety, every question, and every milestone in the upgrading journey from the inside out.

Over the years, I have helped more than 200 families buy, sell, upgrade, and invest across Singapore — from HDB heartland flats to luxury condominiums — consistently achieving outstanding results including record-breaking sale prices and savings my clients never thought possible.

My 3 Guiding Principles

FBA — Focus, Believe and Action

Every goal begins with unwavering focus, the belief that it is achievable, and decisive action to make it happen. I apply this to every client challenge I take on.

LTMA — Less Thinking, More Action

Overthinking costs opportunities. Armed with thorough research and deep market knowledge, I act decisively and timely on behalf of every client I serve.

SMRT — Skill Sets, Mind Sets, Rehearsal & Tool Sets

Continuous learning and preparation is the foundation of exceptional service. I invest in sharpening every dimension of my expertise so my clients benefit from the very best.
