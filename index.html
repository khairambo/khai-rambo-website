About Khai Rambo

Your Trusted Advisor.
For Life.

Khai Rambo is a Senior Associate Director at PropNex Realty Pte Ltd (CEA Reg: R056070J), Singapore's largest real estate agency. With over 10 years in real estate and banking & finance, he has built a reputation as one of Singapore's most trusted property consultants.

Part of the Powerful Negotiators Group — one of the largest teams in Singapore with over 3,000 agents — Khai brings unrivalled market intelligence, proven financial strategies, and genuine care for every client's future.

His mission: to help Singaporeans make the right property decisions — whether buying, selling, renting, or building a powerful investment portfolio — while securing a better future for their families.
